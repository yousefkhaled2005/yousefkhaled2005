### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

Senior AI Systems Architect with 20+ years of applied software engineering and machine learning experience. Specialized in architecting **enterprise-scale LLM platforms**, **agentic orchestration frameworks (LangGraph)**, **hybrid RAG systems (Qdrant / BM25)**, and **high-throughput inference engines (vLLM / Triton)**. Graduated with Honours from the Faculty of Engineering, Benha University.

- 🏛️ **Architecture & Scale**: Designed distributed AI systems serving **50,000+ daily queries** with P95 latency < 450ms.
- 🎯 **LLM Alignment & Fine-Tuning**: Expert in QLoRA 4-bit SFT, Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and domain-specific dataset curation.
- 🚀 **Full-Stack & MLOps**: End-to-end containerized microservices (FastAPI, React/TypeScript, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, GCP, AWS).
