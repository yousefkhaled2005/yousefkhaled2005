### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

Senior AI Systems Architect with 20+ years of continuous applied software engineering and machine learning experience. Graduated with Honours from the **Faculty of Engineering, Benha University (1999–2004)**, specializing in control theory, digital signal processing, and neural network algorithms.

Over the past two decades, navigated each major computing paradigm shift: from **systems programming in C/C++** and **classical statistical modeling**, through the **deep learning revolution (CNNs/LSTMs)**, up to modern **Transformer architectures**, **Enterprise LLMs**, and **Agentic Systems (LangGraph, vLLM, Qdrant)**.

- 🏛️ **Scale & Architecture**: Architect of distributed LLM and RAG platforms serving **50,000+ daily queries** with verified P95 latency < 450ms.
- 🎯 **LLM Alignment & MLOps**: Specialization in instruction-tuning (QLoRA 4-bit SFT), preference alignment (DPO/TRL), hallucination diagnostics, and containerized deployment (Docker, Kubernetes).
- 🔬 **Research & Benchmarks**: Published empirical evaluations comparing vLLM, llama.cpp, and Triton; author of curated Arabic dialect and preference datasets on Hugging Face Hub.
